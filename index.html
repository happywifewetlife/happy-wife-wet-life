The heart of it

Inspired by my husband’s love.

Happy Wife, Wet Life began as a phrase with a whole little ocean tucked inside it. It is for women who feel most alive near water, whether that means fishing from a boat, chasing waves, hauling coolers, rinsing sand out of everything, or wearing the same sun-faded favorite shirt all weekend.

This brand is active, playful, coastal, and personal. A love letter in salt spray.
